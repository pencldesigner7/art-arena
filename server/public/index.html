Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Art Arena collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers the Art Arena web app (the "Service") — including the public pages, your account, art battles and matchmaking.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Username and email address — used to create and sign you in to your account.
  • Password — stored only as a bcrypt hash. We cannot read your password, and it never appears in any API response.
  • Display name — the name other artists see.

Optional profile information

  • A short bio (up to 300 characters) and an avatar picture, if you choose to add them. The avatar is stored on our server so we can display it in rooms and battles.

Google sign-in (optional)

  • If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your Google account ID, Google email address and display name to create or sign you in to your account. We do not receive or store your Google password, and we do not post anything to your Google account.

YouTube connection for LIVE (optional)

  • If you use "Go Live via YouTube", we connect to your YouTube channel so you can create and manage live broadcasts from eligible battles. We store your channel ID, channel name and channel picture, plus authorization tokens that are kept server-side only — tokens are never returned to your browser.
  • For broadcasts you create we store the details you provide (title, privacy setting, scheduled start) and the broadcast status YouTube reports, so streams can be linked to battles and listed on the LIVE page. Art Arena does not transmit or store video today.
  • You can disconnect YouTube at any time — this revokes our access at Google and deletes the connection.

Battle activity

  • Art Arena records the battle activity needed to run the Service: rooms you create or join (room name and settings), matchmaking entries, the battles you take part in, their results, your battle history and your statistics (such as battles played).

One-time email codes

  • When you register or reset your password, we email you a one-time code. Codes are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, expire quickly, and are never returned by our API.

Technical data stored on your device

  • A session token (in sessionStorage and a single HttpOnly cookie) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Your theme choice (Dark or Light), in localStorage.

How we use your information

We use your information only to operate the Service: creating and securing your account, verifying your email, sending password reset codes, matching you with opponents, running and timing battles, recording results, showing your profile in rooms and battles, and displaying in-app notifications about your battles.

What other users can see

Your username, display name, avatar and battle participation/results are visible to other users inside battle rooms, matchmaking and the public LIVE BATTLES list. Public room names are shown to everyone. Your email address and password are never shown to other users.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising, analytics or third-party trackers — the app loads no tracking scripts and sets no advertising cookies.
  • We do not send marketing or newsletter emails. The only emails the Service sends are verification and password-reset codes.

Where your data is stored

The Service and its database run on our cloud hosting provider (Render). Uploaded avatars are stored on the server. Basic, standard hosting-platform server logs (for example request times) exist for security and troubleshooting only.

Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, one-time codes are stored only as hashes, and sessions are kept server-side with expiry. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your password and sign out on shared devices.

Retention and your choices

We keep your information while your account exists. You can edit or remove your profile information (display name, bio, avatar) at any time from the Edit Profile panel. There is currently no in-app account deletion — to request correction or deletion of your account data, email pencldesigner@gmail.com and we will action it.

Children

Art Arena is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and the version shown in the app's About page.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email pencldesigner@gmail.com.

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

The agreement

These Terms govern your use of the Art Arena web app (the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the Service.

What Art Arena is

Art Arena coordinates timed art battles: you can create or join battle rooms, get matched with a random artist, receive random challenge elements, pick the drawing application you want to battle in, and see results, battle history and statistics.

Some honesty about what the Service is today: your artwork itself is created in the drawing application you choose — Art Arena coordinates the battle (timing, matchmaking, challenge elements, results) and does not currently collect or store your artwork files. Features marked "coming soon" in the interface are not part of the Service yet, and the Service involves no payments, prizes or money.

LIVE and YouTube broadcasts

If you connect your YouTube account, a broadcast you create is a real broadcast on your own YouTube channel: Art Arena manages its metadata (title, privacy setting, schedule), links it to your battle and tracks its status. Creating a broadcast is not streaming — Art Arena does not transmit, record or store video today; sending your screen or audio to YouTube is a future feature and, until then, requires your own encoder. Broadcasts are also subject to YouTube's own terms, and going live from Art Arena requires an eligible battle.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.
  • One person, one account — don't create accounts for others or share yours.

Conduct

  • attempt to access another person's account, or probe, scan or test the Service's security without permission;
  • use the Service for anything illegal.

Battles and results

Battle rooms are governed by the settings their host chooses (visibility, mode, time limit and similar). Results are recorded as configured for each battle; if a battle's clock runs out without a recorded result, the battle is recorded as a draw. We may correct plainly erroneous data (for example statistics clearly produced by a glitch).

Suspension and termination
